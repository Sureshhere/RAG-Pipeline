Machine learning (ML) is a subfield of artificial intelligence (AI) focused on developing algorithms that allow computers to learn from data and make decisions without being explicitly programmed. Instead of relying on rigid, pre-defined instructions to solve a problem, machine learning systems are exposed to large amounts of data where they autonomously discover hidden patterns and relationships. As the system processes more information, it adjusts its internal mathematical rules to continuously improve its accuracy and performance over time—much like a human gains expertise through experience. Today, this technology powers widespread digital services, including the recommendation engines on platforms like Netflix, automated speech recognition, predictive text, and financial fraud detection.
